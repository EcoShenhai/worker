Privacy policy

Last updated 30 September 2026 · Shenhai Enterprises Limited · worker@theshenhai.com

Worker (worker.eshcloud.com) is operated by Shenhai Enterprises Limited, the data controller for the personal data described here. Questions or requests: worker@theshenhai.com.

What we collect

  • Account data: name, email address, phone number if you give it, and your EcoID identifier.
  • Service data: organisation, staff and task data you add.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser and device details in security logs.
  • Payment records when you buy a plan: plan, amount, date and payment reference. Card details are handled by the payment provider and never reach us.

Why we use it

  • To provide the service you signed up for (contract).
  • To keep accounts secure and prevent fraud (legitimate interest).
  • To meet legal duties such as tax records (legal obligation).
  • To let you sign in with Google, Facebook or Telegram, only if you choose to (consent).

Who we share it with

  • EcoID, the Shenhai identity service, to sign you in.
  • EcoPay, the Shenhai payments service, with PayPal and Safaricom M-Pesa, to process plan payments.
  • DigitalOcean, which hosts our servers and database.
  • Google, Facebook or Telegram, only if you choose to sign in with them.

We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Account and service data: while your account is active, then deleted or anonymised within 30 days after you delete your account.
  • Invoices and payment records: for the period tax law requires.
  • Security logs: up to 12 months.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, object to processing, or withdraw consent at any time. Email worker@theshenhai.com and we will respond within 30 days. You can also complain to your data protection authority, such as the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner in Kenya.

International transfers

Our providers may process data outside your country. We rely on contractual safeguards with each provider.
